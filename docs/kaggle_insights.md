## How to find the notebooks

1. Go to https://www.kaggle.com/c/santander-customer-transaction-prediction
2. Click the **Code** tab
3. Sort by **Most Votes**
4. Pick **3 notebooks from the top 10** that **differ in approach** — don't pick 3 LightGBM notebooks. Mix it up:
   - One that focuses on feature engineering
   - One that focuses on model choice/tuning
   - One that focuses on EDA insights or competition quirks

Spend ~45 minutes per notebook. Take screenshots of any chart that looks useful for our presentation.

## What to look for in each notebook

- What baseline score did they get?
- What feature engineering did they try? (row-wise stats, "magic" features, anything else)
- Did they handle the synthetic test rows? How?
- What model did they use? What hyperparameters worked?
- Did they do anything with prediction thresholds?
- Any quirks of the data they uncovered?
