# Reflection — Running Log

A log of what we're learning as we go. Whenever something surprises us, breaks, or works really well, we add a note. At the end of the project, this becomes the raw material for our team retrospective.

## How to add an entry

```
### [Day] — Short label
What happened: ...
What we learned: ...
What to do about it: ...
```

---

### [Day 1] — Project kickoff
What happened: First team meeting. Roles set, board built, M1 doc shared with the cohort.
What we learned: [fill in after the kickoff — e.g., "the kickoff took longer than planned" or "we had different ideas about what 'baseline' means"]
What to do about it: [fill in after the kickoff]
